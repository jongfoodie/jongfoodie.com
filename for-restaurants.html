One rule

A score is never for sale.

Not with a partnership, not with a free meal. I visit like any other guest and pay my own bill. When something is paid, it says Ad. That's why guests trust the score, and why it's worth showing.

Not on Strong Foodie yet?

I choose where I go, and I always pay. However, tips are welcome. Like a new opening, a dish people travel for, or a place I shouldn't miss.
